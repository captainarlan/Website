Hello. I'm
Captain Arlan Stagg.

Thirty years on San Francisco Bay, and I still love being out on it. I know these waters, the tides, the wind and the weather, and I've spent more than seven years in command. Today I'm a Director of Operations and lead captain, responsible for vessel inspections, maintenance, scheduling, vendor management and compliance.

Captain Arlan Stagg is a United States Coast Guard licensed 200-Ton Near Coastal Master. I run powerboats for owners around the Bay, look after their boats while they're away, train owners to feel at home at the helm, help clients choose their next boat, and officiate wedding ceremonies aboard or on land. Whatever brings you here, my goal is simple: you relax, enjoy your time on the water, and know you're in good hands.
