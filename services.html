Get comfortable running your own boat.

Personal, hands-on training aboard your own powerboat, at your dock or marina. Whether you've just bought a new boat or want to know your vessel's equipment and handling better, we'll work at your pace, on your boat, with your own equipment.

Training for insurance requirements. If your insurance company asks you to log time with a licensed captain, training can be tailored to your insurer's captain-training requirements, with customized hours and documentation of the instruction you complete. Your insurer makes the final decision on approval.

  • Docking and close-quarters handling
  • Day and overnight anchoring
  • Knots and dock lines
  • Charting and electronics
  • Navigation rules, lights and sound signals
  • Running at speed and at night
  • Bay weather, tides and currents
  • Aids to navigation on the Bay
  • VHF radio use
  • Fueling and preventive maintenance
  • Coast Guard required safety equipment
  • Emergency procedures and man overboard

California’s Boater Card requirement now applies across all age groups to operators of motorized vessels on California waterways, with certain exemptions. The card is obtained through the state’s approved boating-safety education and application process. My hands-on instruction complements that education; it does not replace the approved course and exam or issue a California Boater Card. California Boater Card requirements and approved courses
