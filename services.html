Get comfortable running your own boat.

Personal, hands-on training aboard your own powerboat, at your dock or marina. Whether you've just bought a new boat or want to know your vessel's equipment and handling better, we'll work at your pace, on your boat, with your own equipment.

Training for insurance requirements. If your insurance company asks you to log time with a licensed captain, training can be tailored to your insurer's captain-training requirements, with customized hours and documentation of the instruction you complete. Your insurer makes the final decision on approval.

A day on the Bay with the people you love.

Private charters are arranged through my network of Bay Area marine contacts, aboard vessels appropriately authorized to carry passengers. Tell me your group size, the occasion and how long you'd like to be out, and I'll help match you with the right boat.

Trips can range from a relaxed cruise along the San Francisco waterfront to a run under the Golden Gate Bridge or across to Angel Island. Sea lions, harbor seals and porpoises are regular sights, and humpback whales visit near the Golden Gate in season.
